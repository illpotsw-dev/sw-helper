# 🔴 Red Republic

**Government:** Federal Republic
**Power Tier:** Super Power
**Religion:** Holy Religion (State-aligned)
**Status:** Former Empire turned Republican Superpower

## Overview

The Red Republic is the successor state to one of the most powerful empires in recent history. For centuries, Red dominated vast territories through imperial administration, military might, and religious legitimacy. That age has ended. In recent decades, Red has transitioned — sometimes violently — into a republican system of government, shedding imperial rule in favor of federal institutions and elected leadership.

This transformation culminated only fifteen years ago in a devastating civil war. Veteran-led separatists, hardened by years of imperial service and disillusioned with the central government, broke away to form what is now the Browncoat Confederation, seizing the strategically vital Serenity Valley region. The conflict was bloody, personal, and unresolved. Many officers, politicians, and citizens on both sides are veterans of the war, and memories of the fighting remain raw.

Despite these losses, the Red Republic has emerged as a modern economic superpower. Its industry is vast, its population large, and its capacity to mobilize resources unmatched by nearly any other nation. Red maintains enormous land forces and a sizable navy — aging, but still capable of projecting power when properly supported and modernized.

Red remains a firm supporter and protector of the Holy Religion, which continues to shape public life, political legitimacy, and foreign policy. Faith, nationalism, and republican ideals coexist uneasily, producing a state that is powerful, proud, and still healing from internal fracture.

## Strengths

- Massive IP generation and industrial capacity
- Ability to field and sustain very large armies
- Strong rail and land-based logistics network
- Capable, if aging, blue-water navy
- Large population base for mobilization
- Religious cohesion reinforces national identity

## Weaknesses

- Civil war remains within living memory
- Ongoing tension and unresolved hostility with the Browncoat Confederation
- Veterans' politics and regional loyalties complicate governance
- Naval forces require modernization to remain dominant
- Large borders create multiple strategic fronts

## Strategic Considerations

Playing Red means managing power under strain. The civil war is not history — it is context. Decisions regarding the Browncoats, veterans' organizations, religious policy, and military reform all carry political weight. A mismanaged war or unpopular treaty could reopen internal divisions.

Red's navy is strong enough to matter immediately, but its age means choices must be made between refitting existing ships or committing to new construction. Red excels at prolonged land campaigns, but only if social stability is maintained at home.

## Recommended Playstyle

The Red Republic is best suited for players who enjoy:

- Managing large, complex states
- Balancing internal politics with external power
- Logistics-heavy land warfare
- Navigating post–civil war tensions
- Making decisions that shape the global order

> Red rewards players who understand that victory abroad depends on unity at home.
