# 🐺 Clan McGreggor

**Government:** Clan Warchiefdom
**Power Tier:** Middle Power
**Religion:** Pagan (Cult of Woden)
**Status:** Greenlander Warrior Clan & Rising Energy Power

## Overview

Clan McGreggor is one of the most powerful warrior clans on Greenland, the vast island dominating the far northeastern reaches of the map. Long divided by blood feuds, pagan rivalries, and tribal warfare, Greenland has recently become far more valuable than the mainland ever suspected.

The discovery of vast reserves of high-quality, energy-dense coal across Greenland has fundamentally altered the balance of power. With this discovery, the Warchief of Clan McGreggor now seeks to leverage Greenland's resources — through diplomacy, intimidation, or outright conquest — to seize dominance over the island and transform the clan into a continental economic force.

McGreggor society is built on war, ritual, and blood. The clan worships Woden, a pagan god of battle and sacrifice. Blood sacrifices are common, and blood feuds are a normal and accepted mechanism of justice across Greenland. Authority flows from strength, reputation, and victory in battle, not from bureaucracy or law.

Militarily, Clan McGreggor is defined by the Highland Charge. This is not merely a tactic — it is a cultural identity. Clan chieftains and officers always lead from the front, fighting alongside their warriors. There are no staff officers, no formal planning corps, and little patience for distant command. Leadership is instinctive, personal, and brutally direct.

Any attempt to modernize or shift McGreggor doctrine away from the Highland Charge will be costly, destabilizing, and politically dangerous. The tactic is woven deeply into clan identity, religion, and prestige. Success brings glory; failure invites challenge.

At sea, Clan McGreggor maintains a robust fleet of small craft and riverine vessels, well suited for Greenland's coasts, fjords, and river systems. However, these ships are poorly built by continental standards and ill-suited for blue-water operations. To dominate Greenland fully, the clan will need to either massively expand its tribal fleet or purchase modern ships from foreign powers — a humiliating but potentially lucrative necessity.

Greenland is already contested. The Red Republic maintains a foothold on the island, representing both a threat and an opportunity. That presence must be confronted — either diplomatically or through war. Other great powers may soon follow. It would not take much in the way of foreign weapons, gold, or advisors to turn rival clans against McGreggor, plunging Greenland into proxy conflict.

## Strengths

- Fierce warrior culture with extremely high morale
- Officers lead from the front, inspiring loyalty and aggression
- Strong position in newly discovered coal resources
- Excellent knowledge of Greenland's terrain and rivers
- Capable coastal and riverine forces
- Cultural cohesion rooted in faith and tradition

## Weaknesses

- Rigid, instinct-driven military doctrine
- No professional staff or planning apparatus
- Naval forces are poorly built and technologically backward
- Highly vulnerable to foreign interference and proxy wars
- Internal blood feuds can spiral out of control
- Difficult transition to modern warfare methods

## Strategic Considerations

Playing Clan McGreggor means embracing controlled chaos. Your strength lies in decisive action, aggression, and momentum. Prolonged, methodical wars favor your enemies. Political maneuvering must be backed by the credible threat of violence.

Greenland's coal gives you leverage — but also paints a target on your back. You must decide whether to:

- Unite Greenland through conquest
- Manipulate clan politics and blood feuds
- Court foreign powers carefully
- Or drive all outsiders from the island entirely

The Red Republic's presence is a test. How you respond will shape how the world views McGreggor — either as a rising power or a useful pawn.

## Recommended Playstyle

Clan McGreggor is best suited for players who enjoy:

- Aggressive, high-risk military play
- Roleplay-heavy leadership and culture
- Fast, decisive conflicts
- Managing internal instability
- Turning raw resources into power

> Clan McGreggor does not plan wars.
> They unleash them.
